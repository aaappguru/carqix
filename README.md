# CarQix US — US Automotive Companion Platform

CarQix US is a comprehensive web portal for searching used cars, valuing vehicles, estimating loan payments and taxes, checking VIN histories, comparing auto insurance, and accessing buyer guides.

## Features Ported:
- **Search & Marketplaces**: Multi-marketplace search launcher across Edmunds, CarsDirect, TrueCar, CarGurus, Autotrader, Cars.com, CarMax, Carvana, Bring a Trailer, and Hemmings.
- **Selling & Valuation**: Instant valuation estimator, True Market Value® appraisal portal, and instant cash offer comparisons.
- **Official VIN History & Recalls**: 17-digit VIN decoder with checks across EpicVIN, VinAudit, and official NHTSA safety recalls.
- **Auto Financing & Insurance**: Real-time rate comparisons with Bankrate, Insurify (#1 rate finder), and AAA 24/7 roadside assistance.
- **10+ Interactive Calculators**: Auto Loan, Depreciation, Fuel Cost, State Sales Tax & Out-the-Door, 20/4/10 Affordability, 5-Year TCO, EV vs Gas Savings, Trade-In Equity, Lease vs Buy, and Insurance Estimator.
- **Guides & Buying Advice**: 10-point pre-purchase inspection checklist, Lemon Law advice, title verification, and article library.
- **Client Persistence**: Full bookmarking and search history management stored securely in browser `localStorage`.

## Development & Build
- `npm run dev`: Starts local Vite development server on port 3000
- `npm run build`: Typechecks and compiles production bundle to `dist/`
