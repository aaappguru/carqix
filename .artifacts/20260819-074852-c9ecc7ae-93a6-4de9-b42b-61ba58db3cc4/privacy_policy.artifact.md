# Privacy Policy for CarQix US

**Last Updated: August 19, 2026**

At **CarQix US**, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
*   **Device Information:** We may collect basic information about your device, such as model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to ensure app stability and performance.
*   **Usage Data:** We collect information on how you interact with the app (e.g., which buttons you click, which services you visit) to improve our features.
*   **Preferences:** We store your "Saved Items" and favorite car listings locally on your device using encrypted storage.

## 2. Third-Party Services
CarQix US acts as a hub that connects you to third-party automotive services (e.g., Edmunds, Cars.com, NHTSA).
*   **External Links:** When you click on a car listing or service, you are redirected to a third-party website via Chrome Custom Tabs. These websites have their own privacy policies. We do not control or take responsibility for the data collected by these third parties.
*   **Ads:** We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. Google may use device identifiers to personalize ads.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the collected data to:
*   Provide and maintain the app’s functionality.
*   Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience.
*   Personalize content and advertisements.
*   Communicate app updates or support responses.

## 4. Data Storage
Most of your personal data (like saved cars) is stored **locally on your device**. If you uninstall the app, this local data may be deleted unless backed up by your operating system’s cloud services.

## 5. Your Rights
Depending on your location (e.g., CCPA in California), you may have the right to:
*   Request access to the data we have about you.
*   Request deletion of your data.
*   Opt-out of personalized advertisements through your device settings.

## 6. Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
